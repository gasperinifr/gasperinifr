## Projects

### [Lexiona](https://lexiona.org) · lesson planning platform for teachers

The teacher registers the academic term, the classes and each course's timetable, and the system generates the whole semester of lessons already laid out on the calendar, skipping holidays. Every lesson carries a plan, attendance and history. Five AI agents read the syllabus from PDF, Word, image or audio and generate the lesson plans, suggest alternative approaches, discuss the course and replan whatever content is still pending, all of them receiving the class's actual history as context.

Concept, product, design and development are mine, from the database to the interface. Free end to end: every service it uses has a permanent free tier, and the system was designed to fit inside them.

- The calendar is the product: a course timetable becomes a full semester of lessons, with movable holidays computed rather than typed in.
- Isolation between teachers is enforced inside the database, by Row Level Security, not by the interface.
- Accessibility built into the structure: theming, high contrast, density, text scaling from 80% to 160% and full keyboard navigation.
- Installable PWA on its own domain.

`React 18` `TypeScript` `PostgreSQL` `Supabase` `Deno` `Groq` `TailwindCSS` `PWA`

[![Live](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live-lexiona.org-3FCF8E?style=for-the-badge&logo=googlechrome&logoColor=white)](https://lexiona.org)
